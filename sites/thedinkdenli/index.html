About The Den

Your Court, Your Way

The Dink Den was built around a simple idea: your court, your rules. Round up your crew for a game night, book a few courts for a company outing, or just grab some friends and hang. Play pickleball, catch the game on TV, kick back in the lounge. Our Pro Cushion X courts are the best on Long Island, and the space has couches, a kitchenette, and everything you need to make it feel like your own place. Open 24/7, 365 days a year.
