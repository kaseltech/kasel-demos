2-Week Rules

Non-negotiable

CUT COMPLETELY for 2 Weeks

  • Sugar — all of it. No sodas, candy, desserts, sweetened coffee
  • Processed food — fast food, frozen meals, chips, crackers
  • Bread / pasta / rice
  • Alcohol
  • Artificial sweeteners — they disrupt gut bacteria too
  • Seed oils (canola, soybean, vegetable oil) — cook with olive oil or butter

Daily Supplements

  • Probiotic — 50+ billion CFU, morning with food
  • Psyllium husk — 1 tbsp in water, before bed
  • Magnesium glycinate — 400mg, before bed
  • Electrolytes (LMNT) — 1-2 packets/day, especially first week
  • Water — 100oz minimum

Remember

  • Days 1-5 will suck — that's the sugar withdrawal + gut die-off. It passes.
  • Fermented food with EVERY meal — this is what resets your microbiome
  • Protein at every meal — 200g/day keeps you full and preserves muscle
  • Walk 30 min every day — non-negotiable, even if you feel tired
  • No cotton socks for walking — merino or synthetic only
  • Salt your food generously — you'll flush sodium cutting carbs
